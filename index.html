Seeds Café

Before leaving the Visitor’s Centre and exploring the gardens, why not enjoy our hot drinks and delicious food selection? There are pastries that are homemade and locally sourced sweet treats that change daily.

Stables Kitchen

Using local ingredients that are in season, we prepare a selection of hot food for you to enjoy. It is perfect for relaxing, enjoying lunch, and discussing what you want to do afterwards. We also have fresh salads and a selection of children’s lunchboxes.

Stables Pantry

We offer snacks that boost your energy together with locally sourced cakes and pastries. There are always grab-and-go drinks as well as a selection of hot drinks.

Open Every Day: 1000 - 1700
